# Output Template: Final Memo Format

Follow this structure exactly. Keep the section numbers and order. Never omit a section because data is missing; mark the missing item unknown and proceed.

## Header block
One compact line or table with: ticker | listing/exchange | currency | price (with date) | analysis cutoff | forecast window | mode (Full setup / Pre-earnings / Post-earnings / Thesis update / Comparison or screen / Monitoring design).

## 1. Verdict (dated)
State on one line: setup classification + EPS state (U2/U1/N/D1/D2) + narrative score (with momentum and half-life tags).
Follow with one paragraph: the thesis, the dominant variable, where the view disagrees with the market, and the catalyst that resolves the disagreement. Date the verdict. Do not open with company background.

## 2. Market exam
Fill in the sentence from workflow step 1: "Over the next 12 months, the stock must prove ___ while avoiding ___; current pricing appears to assume ___."
Add 1–3 lines on what changed the exam and separate the company's long-run quality from the stock's current question.

## 3. Expectations baseline
| Item | Value | As of | Source |
|------|-------|-------|--------|
Rows: reported results vs consensus and guidance; forward EPS now vs 30/90/180 days ago with revision breadth; guidance vs consensus vs whisper (mark whisper unknown when unavailable — never invent it); forward valuation with the EPS basis stated; absolute and relative performance; crowding or sentiment proxies; implied good-news and bad-news hurdles from recent reactions.

## 4. Core-variable ledger
| Variable | Last 3–5 period trail | Direction + acceleration + surprise vs expectations | Lead/lag status | Causal EPS link | Durability / half-life | Evidence state | Next observation + thresholds |
|----------|----------------------|------------------------------------------------------|-----------------|-----------------|------------------------|----------------|-------------------------------|
3–7 rows. Lead/lag status is one of: leading, coincident, lagging. Evidence state is one of: verified, supported, open/testable, unfalsifiable, disproved. Every row's last column names a date and explicit confirm / weakens / falsifies levels. Distinguish level effects from growth-rate effects in the direction column.

## 5. EPS revision bridge
| Driver | Direction + probability | Magnitude range | Timing (0–3 / 3–6 / 6–12M) | Persistence | Quality | In consensus? |
|--------|------------------------|-----------------|----------------------------|-------------|---------|---------------|
Close the section with the state assignment (U2/U1/N/D1/D2), the variable that dominates the distribution, and residual tail risks. Do not average independent positives and negatives.

## 6. Narrative diagnosis
- Score: +2 to −2, per the framework definitions.
- Momentum: improving / stable / worsening.
- Half-life: event-driven / medium-cycle / structural.
- Burden of proof: who owns it and why.
- Confirmation status: financially confirmed / financially contradicted / multiple-only.
- Evidence that confirms the narrative; evidence that breaks it.

## 7. Pricing and positioning
Reverse-engineer the current price into required growth, margin, cash flow, or EPS outcomes; state the method and compare the hurdle with the ledger.
Then answer the three questions:
1. What must happen for the stock to rise despite no EPS upgrade?
2. What must happen for the stock to fall despite no EPS downgrade?
3. Which plausible outcome would surprise the market most?
Note crowding, liquidity, event gaps, and borrow constraints when relevant.

## 8. Catalyst path
| Date / window | Catalyst | Consensus or implied hurdle | Expected EPS effect | Expected narrative effect | Market sensitivity | Confirm / weaken / falsify |
|---------------|----------|-----------------------------|---------------------|---------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------|
Every row carries a date or dated window across the 3/6/12-month sequence. Reject undated catalysts. If the evidence arrives after the investment horizon, state the timing mismatch here.

## 9. Scenarios
| Scenario | Probability | EPS revision range | Narrative state | Valuation regime | Key catalysts |
|----------|-------------|--------------------|-----------------|------------------|---------------|
Bull / base / bear. Probabilities sum to 100% and are labeled subjective. Build scenarios from EPS revision ranges, narrative states, valuation regimes, and catalysts — not unsupported price targets.

## 10. Update rule
- Single most important leading indicator: ___
- Next two evidence dates: ___ and ___
- Threshold for moving one EPS grade up or down: ___
- Condition that changes the narrative score: ___
- Explicit thesis breaker: ___
- Still unknowable: ___
For a thesis update, show prior view → new evidence → revised view. Do not rewrite history.

## Formatting rules
- Use ranges, not false precision. Give magnitude bands, not point estimates.
- Treat unknown as unknown, not favorable. Mark missing consensus, whisper, or disclosure explicitly.
- Cite every material current claim near the claim, with exact dates, units, and currencies.
- Keep reported fact, management claim, consensus, whisper, and analyst inference visibly distinct.
- Use one consistent EPS basis throughout and name it (GAAP/IFRS, company-adjusted, or analyst-normalized core); reconcile share count, tax, interest, FX, one-offs, and stock compensation when material.
- Lead with the verdict. Keep the memo in English.
